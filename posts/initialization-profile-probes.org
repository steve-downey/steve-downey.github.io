These are the probes that go with [[link://slug/the-initialization-profile][the review of P4222R3's examples]]: small programs that ask what the two implementations of the initialization profile do with shapes the paper does not show. The compilers and flags are the ones described there, =-std=c++23 -pedantic-errors=, with the profile turned on by the attribute at the top of the file.

{{{TEASER_END}}}

Each section is one Compiler Explorer link with both compilers side by side. The linked source asks the question; the comments here give the answer, line by line and compiler by compiler. A few of the linked sources have a function or two more than is shown here. Two abbreviations run through the comments: FN marks a line that is accepted although it reads, or constructs a second time, memory in the wrong state; FP marks a line that is rejected although the paper says it is fine.

Its comments were written on 2026-10-24 and rechecked on 2026-11-14, after Compiler Explorer had rebuilt the GCC branch at a newer commit. All six sources were compiled again and each comment compared with the diagnostics. Two comments changed, and each says what it said before.
